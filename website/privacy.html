Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026 | Effective for RailPlus Web & Android App

⚠️ Government & Railway Disclaimer (Crucial Notice)

RailPlus is an independent travel assistance application. RailPlus is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, sponsored by, or endorsed by Indian Railways, IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation), CRIS (Centre for Railway Information Systems), or the Government of India. All trademarks, logos, and brand names belong to their respective owners. Official ticket bookings and final travel confirmations can be performed directly on official government websites (irctc.co.in and indianrail.gov.in).

1. Information We Collect

RailPlus is designed to protect your privacy and reduce data collection to the absolute minimum necessary to provide train information services:

  • Search Information: 10-digit PNR numbers, train numbers, station names/codes, travel dates, and class preferences entered by you to perform real-time enquiries.
  • Passenger Master: Passenger name, age, gender, and berth preferences saved optionally by you. This data is isolated per login account or stored in your browser's local sandbox to save time during repeat enquiries.
  • Device & App Diagnostics: Standard technical information such as browser type, operating system version, and general error logs to diagnose app crashes and ensure smooth performance.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

RailPlus follows strict security principles:

  • We NEVER ask for, store, or transmit your IRCTC login password or OTP.
  • We NEVER ask for or store your Aadhaar number, PAN card, or personal identity documents.
  • We NEVER ask for or store debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, or banking credentials. Any official payments are routed securely to authorized payment gateways or official railway portals.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided by users is used solely to:

  • Fetch live PNR current status, train live running status, schedule, and seat availability through third-party railway data APIs (such as RailKit).
  • Provide travel time calculation, delay notifications, and station timelines.
  • Save your preferred passenger list locally for quick enquiry filling.

4. Data Security

All communication between the RailPlus Android App, website, and our servers is strictly encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption. PNR inquiries and live train checks are processed in real-time in memory and are not stored in permanent databases for surveillance or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

RailPlus interacts with independent railway data service providers (including RailKit API) to query publicly available railway information. These third-party services maintain their own privacy policies governing data handling.

6. Permissions Requested in Android App

  • INTERNET: Required to communicate with the RailPlus servers to display live train schedules, PNR status, and seat availability.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data, preventing crashes and displaying friendly retry messages when offline.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is intended for general public travel planning.
