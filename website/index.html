Important Indian Railways Information

PNR Status Codes & Meanings

  • CNF: Confirmed booking with allotted coach and berth.
  • RAC: Reservation Against Cancellation — guaranteed boarding.
  • WL: Waiting List ticket — confirmable upon cancellation.
  • TQ: Tatkal waiting list (zero refund upon cancellation if confirmed).

Chart Preparation Timeline

  • First Chart: Prepared approx. 4 hours prior to train departure.
  • Final Chart: Prepared ~30 minutes prior from charting station.
  • Railway Helpline: Dial 139 for 24x7 security & medical assistance.
  • Authorized Partner: Prepares journey details and hands off to IRCTC portal.
